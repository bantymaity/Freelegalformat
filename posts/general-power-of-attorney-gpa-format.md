Managing real estate properties, operating bank accounts, collecting rents, attending court hearings, or executing municipal agreements can become extremely challenging if you are an Non-Resident Indian (NRI) living abroad, an elderly senior citizen with mobility constraints, or a busy business executive constantly traveling for work.

When you cannot physically be present to manage your legal, financial, or property matters in India, Indian law allows you to appoint a trusted person to act legally on your behalf.

This legal authorization instrument is known as a **Power of Attorney (POA)**.

Under the **Powers of Attorney Act, 1882**, a Power of Attorney is a formal legal deed by which one person (the **Principal** or Executant) grants legal authority to another trusted individual (the **Attorney**, Agent, or Donee) to perform legal, financial, property, or administrative acts on their behalf.

However, executing a Power of Attorney requires immense caution. Granting overly broad powers to the wrong person can lead to property misuse, unauthorized bank transactions, or legal disputes.

In this practical legal guide, we explain the difference between a **General Power of Attorney (GPA)** and a **Special Power of Attorney (SPA)**, highlight landmark Supreme Court rulings regarding property sales, detail stamp duty and registration rules across Indian states, provide **two free ready-to-use downloadable GPA and SPA formats**, and outline how to safely revoke a POA.

| Parameter | General Power of Attorney (GPA) | Special Power of Attorney (SPA) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Risk Level** | Higher risk; requires complete trust in the appointed agent. | Lower risk; authority cannot be misused for other matters. |
| **Registration Needs** | Registration mandatory if dealing with immovable property rights. | Registration depends on transaction type (compulsory for property sale/lease). |

### Supreme Court Ruling: Can Property Be Sold via GPA in India?

A common legal misconception in India is that a buyer can "purchase" real estate property simply by obtaining a General Power of Attorney from the seller without executing a registered Sale Deed.

In the landmark judgment **Suraj Lamp & Industries Pvt. Ltd. vs. State of Haryana (2011)**, the Supreme Court of India conclusively ruled:

* **A Power of Attorney is NOT an instrument of transfer of title.** An immovable property (land, flat, commercial building) cannot be legally bought or sold through a GPA, Agreement to Sell, or Will.
* Title ownership of real estate can **ONLY** be transferred through a registered **Sale Deed** executed under Section 54 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882.
* **What a GPA CAN do:** A registered GPA allows an agent to represent the principal before the Sub-Registrar to execute and sign a Sale Deed on the principal's behalf.

### Template 1: General Power of Attorney (GPA) Format (Property & Banking Management)

Use this format when authorizing a trusted family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling) to manage your real estate properties, bank accounts, municipal taxes, and court representations.

> **GENERAL POWER OF ATTORNEY**
>
> **TO ALL TO WHOM THESE PRESENTS SHALL COME, I:**
>
> **[PRINCIPAL FULL LEGAL NAME]**, S/o, D/o, W/o: **[Father's/Husband's Name]**, aged about **[Age]** years, residing at **[Complete Residential Address]**, holding Aadhaar Card No. **[XXXX-XXXX-XXXX]** and PAN Card No. **[ABCDE1234F]** (hereinafter referred to as the **"PRINCIPAL"**).
>
> **DO HEREBY SEND GREETINGS:**
>
> **WHEREAS** owing to my [state reason, e.g., advanced age / residing abroad / pre-occupation with business], I am unable to personally attend to the management of my movable and immovable properties, banking transactions, and legal affairs in India.
>
> **AND WHEREAS** I have full faith and confidence in my **[relationship, e.g., Son / Spouse / Brother]**, **[ATTORNEY FULL LEGAL NAME]**, S/o: **[Father's Name]**, residing at **[Attorney Residential Address]**, holding Aadhaar Card No. **[XXXX-XXXX-XXXX]** (hereinafter referred to as the **"ATTORNEY"**).
>
> **NOW KNOW YE ALL MEN AND THESE PRESENTS WITNESS** that I, the Principal, do hereby nominate, constitute, and appoint the said Attorney as my true and lawful General Attorney to do, perform, and execute all or any of the following acts, deeds, and things in my name and on my behalf:
>
> **1. PROPERTY MANAGEMENT:**
> To look after, manage, superintend, and maintain all my residential, commercial, and agricultural immovable properties situated anywhere in India. To pay municipal property taxes, utility bills, society maintenance fees, and obtain necessary receipts in my name.
>
> **2. LEASING & RENT COLLECTION:**
> To let out on rent or lease any of my properties to suitable tenants, execute 11-month rent agreements or lease deeds, collect monthly rents, issue valid receipts, and initiate legal proceedings to evict defaulting tenants if necessary.

> **3. BANKING OPERATIONS:**
> To operate my existing Savings, Current, and Fixed Deposit bank accounts maintained with **[Bank Name & Branch Address]** (Account No: **[Account No]**), deposit cheques, draw funds, issue cheques, apply for net banking facilities, and manage term deposits on my behalf.

> **4. LEGAL & COURT REPRESENTATION:**
> To represent me before any Court of Law, Civil Court, Revenue Authority, Consumer Forum, Municipal Corporation, or Government Office; to sign and verify plaints, petitions, written statements, affidavits, and appoint advocates on my behalf.

> **5. TAX & MUNICIPAL MATTERS:**
> To sign and file income tax returns, represent me before Income Tax and GST authorities, and apply for water/electricity connection meter transfers in my name.

> **6. RATIFICATION:**

### Stamp Duty Rates & Registration Mandatory Guidelines

To ensure your Power of Attorney is legally valid and admissible in Indian courts and government offices, strict stamp duty and registration rules apply:

#### 1. Mandatory Registration at Sub-Registrar Office (SRO)
* **When Registration is Compulsory:** Under Section 17 of the Registration Act, 1908, any Power of Attorney that authorizes an agent to sell, transfer, mortgage, or create rights in immovable property **MUST be registered at the Sub-Registrar's Office** where the property is located.
* **Non-Property GPAs:** GPAs for simple administrative tasks, court representations, or banking can be executed on non-judicial stamp paper and notarized by a licensed **Notary Public**.

#### 2. Stamp Duty Value Across Major States:

| State / Region | GPA to Family Member (Spouse, Child, Parent) | GPA to Non-Family / Third Party |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Delhi / NCR** | ₹1,000 to ₹2,000 Stamp Duty + Registration Fee | 5% to 6% Stamp Duty (Equivalent to Sale Deed) |
| **Maharashtra** | ₹500 Stamp Duty under Article 48 | 5% Stamp Duty on Market Value of Property |
| **Karnataka** | ₹1,000 Stamp Duty + Registration Fee | 5% Stamp Duty + Registration Fees |
| **West Bengal** | Nominal Stamp Duty (Family) | Scaled Stamp Duty per Stamp Act |

### How NRIs Can Execute a Power of Attorney Abroad for Use in India

If you are an Non-Resident Indian (NRI) living in the US, UK, Canada, UAE, or Australia and need to execute a POA for property or banking matters in India, follow this official consular process:

[Draft POA Deed] ➔ [Attestation at Indian Embassy / Consulate OR Apostille] ➔ [Send Physical Copy to India] ➔ [Adjudication at District Collector Office within 3 Months]


1. **Step 1: Drafting:** Draft the POA deed on plain A4 paper as per Indian legal requirements.
2. **Step 2: Consulate Attestation / Apostille:** 
   * **Option A:** Sign the POA deed in the physical presence of an Officer at the **Indian Embassy / Consulate General** in your host country, who will affix their official embassy seal and signature.
   * **Option B:** Get the document notarized by a local foreign Notary Public and then **Apostilled** by the host government authority (if the country is a signatory to the Hague Apostille Convention).
3. **Step 3: Dispatch to India:** Send the physical attested/apostilled POA deed via courier to your appointed Attorney in India.
4. **Step 4: Adjudication in India (Mandatory):** Within **3 months (90 days)** of receiving the POA in India, the Attorney must present the document to the **District Collector / District Registrar (Stamp Duty Department)** for **Adjudication and Payment of Local Stamp Duty**. Once adjudicated, the POA gains full legal validity across India.

### How to Revoke or Cancel a Power of Attorney legally

A Principal can cancel or revoke a Power of Attorney at any time, provided they are mentally competent. To legally revoke a POA:

1. **Execute a Deed of Revocation:** Draft a formal **Deed of Revocation of Power of Attorney** on non-judicial stamp paper.
2. **Issue Public Notice in Newspapers:** Publish a public notice in two local newspapers announcing that the POA granted to [Attorney Name] has been cancelled and revoked with immediate effect.
3. **Register the Revocation Deed:** If the original POA was registered at a Sub-Registrar's Office, the Revocation Deed **must also be registered** at the same Sub-Registrar's Office.
4. **Serve Written Notice on Attorney & Banks:** Send a registered post copy of the Revocation Deed to the Attorney, concerned bank branches, sub-registrar offices, and municipal authorities to prevent unauthorized usage.
