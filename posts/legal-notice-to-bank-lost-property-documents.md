### The RBI Master Rule: Rs. 5,000 Per Day Penalty

For decades, banks harassed borrowers by delaying the return of original documents. To stop this, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) issued a strict mandate on **Responsible Lending Conduct**.

According to the RBI directives:
1. **The 30-Day Deadline:** Banks and NBFCs MUST release all original movable/immovable property documents within **30 days** after the full repayment and closure of the loan account.
2. **The Rs. 5,000/Day Penalty:** If the bank fails to return the documents within 30 days, they are legally mandated to pay a penalty of **Rs. 5,000 for each day of delay** directly to the borrower!
3. **Compensation for Loss:** In case the documents are completely lost or damaged, the bank has an additional 30 days to assist the borrower in obtaining duplicate/certified copies. However, the Rs. 5,000 per day penalty still applies to the delay, *plus* the bank is liable to pay separate compensation for the permanent depreciation of the property's value.

*The Supreme Court and the National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (NCDRC) have routinely ordered banks (including giants like SBI, ICICI, and HDFC) to pay compensation ranging from Rs. 2 Lakhs to Rs. 5 Lakhs for losing original property deeds.*

### The "Lost Document Protocol": What the Bank MUST Do (At Their Cost)

If your bank loses your documents, do not run around doing their dirty work. Legally, the bank must complete the following steps entirely at their own expense:

1. **File an FIR:** The bank manager must file an official FIR at the local police station declaring that the bank has lost your specific property documents.
2. **Publish Newspaper Advertisements:** The bank must publish a "Loss of Document" public notice in two leading newspapers (one English and one regional language) stating that the original deed has been lost by them.
3. **Provide an Indemnity Bond:** The bank must issue a registered, notarized Indemnity Bond on heavy stamp paper in your favor. This bond guarantees that if any future financial loss occurs because someone misuses your lost original deed, the bank will bear 100% of the legal and financial liability.
4. **Procure the Certified Copy:** The bank's legal team must apply to the Sub-Registrar’s office, pay the necessary fees, and obtain a Certified True Copy (CTC) of your Sale Deed.

*Only after these 4 steps are completed should you even consider negotiating the final financial compensation.*

### Escalation: What If the Bank Ignores You?

If the bank tries to negotiate and offers you only Rs. 20,000 or Rs. 50,000 as a "goodwill gesture," **do not accept it.** By accepting a small settlement and signing a release form, you waive your right to sue them for the actual loss.

If they fail to comply with your legal notice:
1. **File an RBI Ombudsman Complaint:** Log in to the RBI CMS (Complaint Management System) portal. Attach the legal notice and the NOC. The Ombudsman has the power to order the bank to pay up to Rs. 20 Lakhs in compensation and strictly enforce the Rs. 5,000/day penalty.
2. **File a Consumer Court Case:** For high-value properties (e.g., properties worth Crores), the depreciation value is massive. Hire an advocate and file a case in the State Consumer Court demanding compensation proportional to 20% of your property's current market value. The courts have consistently ruled that banks cannot escape liability just by paying the cost of a certified copy.
