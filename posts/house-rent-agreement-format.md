Renting out a residential flat, independent house, or commercial shop space in India is one of the most reliable sources of monthly rental income. Similarly, for millions of students, young working professionals, and families moving to new cities, finding a rented accommodation is the first step toward settling down.

However, when a tenancy relationship relies solely on verbal promises or informal handshake deals, it frequently leads to bitter disputes—unpaid utility bills, unexpected rent hikes, refusal to refund security deposits, property damage, or long delays when the landlord asks the tenant to vacate.

To ensure complete financial and legal protection for both property owners and tenants, executing a legally binding **House Rent Agreement** (or Lease Deed) is absolute essential.

A Rent Agreement defines the exact terms of tenancy, including monthly rent, security deposit amount, maintenance charges, notice period for eviction, lock-in period, and rules regarding structural alterations.

In this practical legal guide, we explain why rent agreements in India are executed for **11 months**, outline stamp duty rates across major Indian states, provide **two free ready-to-use downloadable Rent Agreement formats** (Standard Residential Draft and Commercial Shop Lease Draft), list 7 essential clauses every landlord and tenant must include, and explain how to get your agreement notarized or registered.

### Why Are Rent Agreements Executed for Exactly 11 Months?

If you have ever rented an apartment in India, you must have noticed that rental contracts are almost universally executed for **11 months** rather than a full 12-month year. There is a specific statutory legal reason behind this convention:

                  [Tenancy Tenure Selection]
                              │
     ┌────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┐
     ▼                                                 ▼
[11-MONTH RENT AGREEMENT]                         [12+ MONTH LEASE DEED]
Governed by Indian Registration Act, Sec 17       Mandatory Registration at Sub-Registrar
Registration NOT compulsory (Notarization ok)     High Stamp Duty + Registration Fees required
Fast, cost-effective, easy renewal                Involves complex legal paperwork


Under **Section 17 of the Indian Registration Act, 1908**, any lease agreement for immovable property that extends for **12 months or longer (1 year or more)** must be mandatorily registered at the local Sub-Registrar’s Office (SRO), incurring substantial stamp duty and court registration fees.

To avoid heavy registration expenses, complex paperwork, and lengthy sub-registrar court visits, landlords and tenants execute an **11-month agreement**. An 11-month agreement does not require compulsory registration under Section 17, saving both parties substantial time and money while remaining fully valid when printed on non-judicial stamp paper and notarized.

### 7 Mandatory Clauses Every Rent Agreement Must Contain

To prevent future legal disputes, ensure your rental contract includes these seven critical legal clauses:

1. **Rent Amount & Payment Due Date:** Specify the exact monthly rent figure, payment due date (e.g., "on or before the 5th of every English calendar month"), and permitted payment modes (UPI, Bank Transfer, Cheque).
2. **Security Deposit & Refund Terms:** Detail the total interest-free security deposit paid, terms for deduction (unpaid rent or damage repairs), and the timeline for full refund upon vacating (usually within 7–15 days).
3. **Lock-in Period:** A mandatory timeframe (typically 3 to 6 months) during which neither the landlord can evict the tenant nor the tenant can vacate without forfeiting their security deposit.
4. **Notice Period for Termination:** Explicitly define the advance notice period required by either party to terminate the tenancy (usually **1 month or 2 months**).
5. **Maintenance & Utility Charges:** Clarify who pays monthly housing society maintenance charges, municipal water bills, and electricity consumption charges.
6. **Property Maintenance & Alterations:** Prohibition against structural modifications, painting, major electrical changes, or subletting the premises to third parties without prior written consent.
7. **Annual Rent Escalation Clause:** Standard clause stating the percentage by which rent will increase upon renewal after 11 months (typically **5% to 10%**).

### Template 1: Standard 11-Month Residential House Rent Agreement Draft

You can copy this universal agreement template, edit your specific details inside the brackets `[ ... ]`, and print it on non-judicial stamp paper.

> **RESIDENTIAL HOUSE RENT AGREEMENT**
>
> This Rent Agreement is made and executed at **[City Name]** on this **[Day]** day of **[Month], 20[Year]**, by and between:
>
> **LESSOR / LANDLORD:**
> **[LANDLORD FULL LEGAL NAME]**, S/o, D/o, W/o: **[Father's/Husband's Name]**, residing at **[Landlord Permanent Address]**, holding Aadhaar Card No. **[XXXX-XXXX-XXXX]** (hereinafter referred to as the **"LESSOR"**, which expression shall include their legal heirs, executors, and assigns);
>
> **AND**
>
> **LESSEE / TENANT:**
> **[TENANT FULL LEGAL NAME]**, S/o, D/o: **[Father's Name]**, permanent resident of **[Tenant Native Address]**, holding Aadhaar Card No. **[XXXX-XXXX-XXXX]** and Mobile No. **[+91-XXXXXXXXXX]** (hereinafter referred to as the **"LESSEE"**, which expression shall include their legal heirs and assigns).
>
> **WHEREAS** the Lessor is the absolute owner of residential property situated at **[Complete Address of Rented Flat/House, Floor, Building Name, City, PIN Code]** (hereinafter referred to as the **"Demised Premises"**).
>
> **AND WHEREAS** at the request of the Lessee, the Lessor has agreed to let out the Demised Premises for residential purposes only for a temporary period of **11 (Eleven) Months** commencing from **[Commencement Date: DD/MM/YYYY]** to **[Expiry Date: DD/MM/YYYY]** on the following mutual terms and conditions:
>
> **NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:**
>
> **1. MONTHLY RENT:**
> The Lessee agrees to pay to the Lessor a monthly rent of **Rs. [Amount in Figures]/- (Rupees [Amount in Words] Only)** per month. The rent shall be payable in advance on or before the **[e.g., 5th]** day of each English calendar month via Bank Transfer / UPI to the Lessor's Bank Account No: **[Landlord Bank Account Details]**.
>
> **2. INTEREST-FREE SECURITY DEPOSIT:**
> The Lessee has paid a sum of **Rs. [Deposit Amount]/- (Rupees [Amount in Words] Only)** to the Lessor as an interest-free refundable security deposit vide Transaction UTR / Cheque No. **[Insert UTR/Cheque No]** dated **[Date]**. This deposit shall be refunded by the Lessor to the Lessee at the time of handing over peaceful vacant possession of the premises, after deducting any outstanding utility bills or structural damage repair costs.
>
> **3. DURATION AND LOCK-IN PERIOD:**
> This agreement is valid for **11 Months** starting from **[Start Date]** to **[End Date]**. Both parties agree to a mandatory **Lock-in Period of [e.g., 3 Months]**. Neither party can terminate the agreement during the lock-in period.
>
> **4. NOTICE PERIOD:**
> After the expiry of the lock-in period, either party may terminate this agreement by giving **1 (One) Month written notice** or paying 1 month's rent in lieu of notice.
>
> **5. UTILITY & MAINTENANCE CHARGES:**
> In addition to monthly rent, the Lessee shall pay monthly electricity charges as per actual meter reading, water charges, and society maintenance fees of **Rs. [Amount]/-** directly to the concerned authority/society.
>
> **6. RESTRICTIONS ON USE & SUBLETTING:**
> The Lessee shall use the Demised Premises strictly for residential purposes for themselves and their family. The Lessee shall not sublet, assign, or transfer the premises or any part thereof to any third party, nor carry out any commercial or unlawful activities inside the premises.
>
> **7. REPAIRS & MAINTENANCE:**
> The Lessee shall keep the interior fittings, electrical switches, and plumbing in good working condition. Day-to-day minor repairs shall be borne by the Lessee, while major structural repairs shall be attended to by the Lessor.
>
> **8. INSPECTION & RENEWAL:**
> The Lessor or their authorized representative shall have the right to enter and inspect the premises at reasonable hours with prior notice. Upon expiry of 11 months, this agreement may be renewed for a further period with a **[e.g., 10%]** escalation in monthly rent, subject to mutual consent.
>

### Stamp Duty Rates & Registration Rules by Major Indian States

Stamp duty rates on rent agreements are governed by state stamp acts. Print your agreement on non-judicial e-stamp paper of appropriate value:

| State / Metro Region | 11-Month E-Stamp Paper Value | Notarization / Registration Mandate |

| **Delhi / NCR** | ₹50 or ₹100 e-Stamp Paper | Notarization recommended |

### 4 Costly Mistakes Landlords & Tenants Must Avoid

1. **Skipping Tenant Police Verification:** Executing a rent agreement without submitting a Tenant Police Verification Form exposes property owners to criminal liability under BNS/IPC.
2. **Not Inventorying Furniture & Fixtures:** Failing to list existing ACs, fans, geysers, and furniture units in a written Annexure sheet attached to the agreement causes end-of-tenancy deposit disputes.
3. **Accepting Cash Without Receipts:** Tenants paying rent in cash should always insist on a signed Rent Receipt to claim House Rent Allowance (HRA) tax deductions under Section 10(13A).
4. **Vague Maintenance Definitions:** Not defining whether society painting or elevator replacement costs belong to the landlord or tenant. Major society corpus fund maintenance is always the landlord's liability.
