### The "15-Day Grievance Rule": Your Legal Launchpad

When your claim is rejected, your first instinct might be to hire an expensive consumer court lawyer. **Stop.** There is a much faster, legally mandated protocol you must follow first. 

Under the **Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI)** regulations, you cannot directly sue the insurance company or approach the Ombudsman without first exhausting their internal Grievance Redressal mechanism.

**The Protocol:**
1. **Send a Formal Notice:** You must send a written Representation/Legal Notice to the Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO) of your specific insurance company.
2. **The 15-Day Clock:** Once the GRO receives your notice, they have exactly **15 days** to resolve the issue, pay the claim, or give a final written rejection. 
3. **The Escalation:** If they ignore your notice for 15 days, or if you are unsatisfied with their reply, the legal doors to the Ombudsman and Consumer Court swing wide open for you.

### Template 1: Legal Notice for Health Insurance Claim Rejection

Use this formal notice format when your health or mediclaim policy is wrongly rejected by the TPA or the insurance company.

> **LEGAL NOTICE / FORMAL REPRESENTATION TO GRO**
>
> **BY SPEED POST & REGISTERED EMAIL**
>
> **Date:** [Insert Date]
>
> **TO:**
> **The Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO),**
> **[Name of Insurance Company, e.g., Star Health / HDFC Ergo]**
> [Corporate Office Address of the Insurance Company]
>
> **SUBJECT: Legal representation against the arbitrary and illegal repudiation (rejection) of Health Insurance Claim No. [Insert Claim Number] under Policy No. [Insert Policy Number].**
>
> **Sir/Madam,**
>
> I, **[Your Full Name]**, residing at **[Your Address]**, am a bonafide policyholder of your company holding the aforementioned health insurance policy since **[Year of Policy Inception]**. I have paid all premiums continuously without any default.
>
> 1. That on **[Date of Hospitalization]**, I/my family member was admitted to **[Hospital Name]** for the medical treatment of **[Name of Disease / Surgery]**. 
> 2. That I was discharged on **[Date of Discharge]** and the total hospital bill amounted to **Rs. [Total Bill Amount]/-**.
> 3. That I duly submitted all original bills, discharge summaries, and diagnostic reports to your TPA desk on **[Date of Submission]** within the stipulated time frame.
> 4. To my utter shock, your company vide email/letter dated **[Date of Rejection]** arbitrarily repudiated my legitimate claim citing the reason: *"[Quote the exact reason given by them, e.g., Concealment of Pre-Existing Disease]"*.
> 5. **Rebuttal of Allegation:** I categorically deny your baseless reasoning. The medical condition for which I was treated was diagnosed for the very first time on **[Date]**, well after the policy inception and waiting period. There is absolutely no previous medical history, and your appointed doctor/surveyor has failed to produce any prior medical record to prove this alleged "pre-existing" condition.
> 6. Your arbitrary rejection constitutes a severe **Deficiency in Service** and an Unfair Trade Practice under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, causing me immense financial hardship and mental agony.
>
> **MY STATUTORY DEMAND:**
> I hereby call upon you to review my case file, overturn the illegal rejection, and release the full claim amount of **Rs. [Claim Amount]/-** directly to my bank account within **15 (Fifteen) Days** from the receipt of this notice.
>
> Take note that if you fail to settle this claim within 15 days, I shall immediately escalate this matter to the **Insurance Ombudsman** and file a formal suit in the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, holding your company liable for the principal amount along with 12% penal interest and heavy compensation for harassment.
>
> *(Signature)*
> **[Your Full Name]**
> **Mobile:** [Your Number]
> **Email:** [Your Email]

### Template 2: Legal Notice for Car/Motor Insurance Claim Rejection

Use this format when your vehicle meets with an accident, but the surveyor rejects the claim citing "Delay in Intimation" or "Outside Scope of Coverage."

> **LEGAL NOTICE FOR REPUDIATION OF MOTOR CLAIM**
>
> **Date:** [Insert Date]
>
> **TO:**
> **The Grievance Redressal Officer,**
> **[Insurance Company Name]**
> [Address]
>
> **SUBJECT: Representation against wrongful rejection of Own-Damage Motor Claim No. [Insert Number] for Vehicle bearing Registration No. [Vehicle Number].**
>
> **Sir/Madam,**
>
> 1. That I am the registered owner of the vehicle **[Car Make & Model]** bearing Reg. No. **[Vehicle Number]**, comprehensively insured with your company vide Policy No. **[Policy Number]** valid from [Date] to [Date].
> 2. That on **[Date of Accident]**, my vehicle met with a severe accident at **[Location]**. 
> 3. That I immediately took the vehicle to the authorized garage and duly intimated your claim department on **[Date of Intimation]**. *(If there was a delay, state the valid reason: e.g., "As I was severely injured and hospitalized, I could only intimate the company after 4 days.")*
> 4. That your surveyor inspected the vehicle, yet your company rejected my claim vide letter dated **[Date]** stating *"[Quote their reason, e.g., Delay in Intimation / Invalid Driving License]"*.
> 5. **Rebuttal:** As per the IRDAI Master Circular and established Supreme Court guidelines, a genuine claim cannot be rejected purely on hyper-technical grounds such as a minor delay in intimation, especially when the accident and damages are verifiable and the driver possessed a valid license at the time of the incident.
>
> **DEMAND:**
> I demand that you process the approved estimate of **Rs. [Repair Amount]/-** and clear the payment to the authorized workshop / my account within **15 Days**. Failure to do so will force me to drag your company to the Insurance Ombudsman and Consumer Court for deficiency in service.
>
> *(Signature)*
> **[Your Full Name]**

### The Ultimate Weapon: The Insurance Ombudsman

If the insurance company ignores your legal notice or sends back a stubborn rejection letter, do not rush to pay a lawyer to file a Consumer Court case just yet. 

The Government of India has created the **Council for Insurance Ombudsmen (CIO)**. This is a quasi-judicial body specifically designed to punish erring insurance companies. 
* **It is 100% Free:** You do not need to pay any court fees.
* **No Lawyer Required:** You can represent yourself and tell your story in simple language.
* **Binding Power:** If the Ombudsman orders the insurance company to pay you, the company is legally bound to comply within 30 days. However, if the Ombudsman rules against you, you still have the absolute right to reject their decision and go to Consumer Court!

**How to File:**
1. Wait for the 15-day notice period to expire.
2. Go to the official **Bima Bharosa Portal** (`bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in`) or physically visit your regional Ombudsman office.
3. Fill out the complaint form (Form VI), attach the rejection letter, and a copy of the Legal Notice you sent to the GRO. 
4. The Ombudsman will arrange a mediation hearing, and in most cases of hyper-technical rejections, they order the company to release the funds.
