You resigned from your job, waited the mandatory 60 days, and successfully submitted your Form 19 (for PF) and Form 10C (for EPS Pension) on the UAN member portal. According to the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) guidelines, claim settlements are supposed to be processed within 7 to 20 working days. 

However, a month has passed. You check your claim status every morning, and it remains stubbornly stuck at **"Under Process"**. 

In other scenarios, your previous employer might be refusing to approve your KYC updates, or your PF transfer from an old company to a new company has been rejected without a clear explanation. 

When you hit a roadblock with your hard-earned retirement savings, waiting endlessly is not the solution. You need to officially escalate the matter.

The EPFO provides a highly effective grievance redressal mechanism known as **EPFiGMS (EPF i Grievance Management System)**. To get a fast response, whether you are filing an online complaint or sending a physical letter to the Regional PF Commissioner, the way you draft your grievance is crucial.

In this practical guide, we explain when to file an EPFO grievance, provide **three free ready-to-use Grievance Letter formats** (for Delayed Withdrawal, Employer KYC Delay, and EPFiGMS Portal copy-paste drafts), and explain how to track your complaint.

---

### 3 Common Scenarios That Require an EPFO Grievance

Do not file a grievance on day two of your claim submission. You should only escalate the matter under these circumstances:

1. **Delayed Claim Settlement:** You submitted a PF withdrawal claim (Form 19, 10C, or 31) and it has been stuck in the "Under Process" stage for more than 20 days.
2. **Employer Non-Cooperation:** Your ex-employer is intentionally delaying the approval of your KYC (Aadhaar/PAN/Bank), exit date (Date of Leaving), or online PF transfer request.
3. **Claim Rejected with Vague Reasons:** Your claim was rejected by the field office with unclear or automated remarks, and you need a specific explanation from the dealing assistant to rectify the error.

### Template 1: Grievance Letter to Regional PF Commissioner (Delayed Withdrawal)

If your online grievance on the EPFiGMS portal was closed without resolution, or if your case is highly complex, you can send a physical application via Speed Post to the Regional PF Commissioner of your specific EPFO field office.

> **APPLICATION FOR EXPEDITING PF CLAIM SETTLEMENT**
>
> **Date:** [Insert Date]
>
> **To,**
> **The Regional P.F. Commissioner,**
> Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO),
> Regional Office: **[Insert Address of your EPFO Regional Office]**
>
> **SUBJECT: Grievance regarding the delay in PF & EPS Withdrawal Claim (Form 19 & 10C) for UAN [Insert 12-Digit UAN].**
>
> **Respected Sir/Madam,**
>
> I, **[Your Full Name]**, am a subscriber to the Employees' Provident Fund holding Universal Account Number (UAN) **[Insert UAN]** and PF Member ID **[Insert Specific PF Account Number]**. 
>
> I previously worked at **[Company Name]** from **[Date of Joining]** to **[Date of Leaving]**.
>
> I submitted my online final withdrawal claims for Form 19 (PF) and Form 10C (Pension) on **[Date of Claim Submission]**. However, it has been over **[Number] days**, and the claim status on the UAN member portal still shows as **"Under Process"**. 
>
> My Claim Tracking ID is: **[Insert Claim ID]**.
>
> All my KYC details (Aadhaar, PAN, and Bank Account) are fully verified and approved by the employer. I am currently facing severe financial hardship and urgently require these funds for **[state a brief reason, e.g., urgent medical treatment / family obligations]**.
>
> I humbly request you to kindly look into this matter, instruct the concerned dealing hand to process my pending claim, and credit the settlement amount to my registered bank account at the earliest.
>
> Thank you for your time, understanding, and quick assistance.
>
> **Enclosures Attached:**
> 1. Copy of Claim Status Screenshot.
> 2. Copy of Aadhaar Card and PAN Card.
> 3. Copy of Cancelled Cheque.
>
> **Yours faithfully,**
>
> *(Signature)*
> **[Your Full Name]**
> **Registered Mobile No:** [+91-XXXXXXXXXX]
> **Email ID:** [Your Email Address]

### Template 2: Notice / Email to HR for Delay in KYC or PF Transfer Approval

Often, the delay is not from the EPFO's side, but from your previous employer's side. If your ex-employer is not approving your online requests using their Digital Signature Certificate (DSC), send them this formal, polite escalation email.

> **Subject: URGENT: Request to approve pending PF Transfer / KYC Request - [Your Name] (Emp ID: [Your Old ID])**
>
> **Dear [HR Manager's Name / HR Department],**
>
> I hope this email finds you well.
>
> I am a former employee of **[Company Name]**, having worked as **[Your Designation]** under Employee ID **[Your ID]**. My last working day was **[Date of Leaving]**.
>
> I am writing to bring to your urgent attention that my **[mention request type: KYC Verification / Date of Exit marking / PF Transfer Request]** is currently pending at the employer's end on the EPFO portal since **[Date of Request]**.
>
> As per EPFO procedures, my PF withdrawal/transfer cannot be processed by the field office until it is digitally approved by the authorized signatory of the company. 
>
> * **My UAN:** [Insert UAN]
> * **My PF Member ID:** [Insert PF Number]
>
> I kindly request you to log into the Employer EPFO portal and approve the pending request at your earliest convenience so I can proceed with the settlement. I have attached a screenshot of the pending status for your reference.
>
> Looking forward to your prompt support and cooperation.
>
> **Best regards,**
>
> **[Your Full Name]**
> **Mobile:** [+91-XXXXXXXXXX]

### Template 3: Copy-Paste Draft for EPFiGMS Online Portal Complaint

Filing a complaint online via **`epfigms.gov.in`** is the fastest way to get your issue resolved. The portal has a character limit for the grievance description. You can copy and paste the text below into the online complaint box:

**For Delayed Withdrawal Claim:**
> "Respected Sir/Madam, My UAN is [Your UAN] and PF No is [Your PF No]. I applied for Form 19 & 10C online on [Date of Application] (Claim ID: [Claim ID]). It has been more than 20 days, but the status is still 'Under Process'. My KYC is fully approved and there is no discrepancy. Please process the claim urgently as I am in deep financial need. Thank you."

**For PF Transfer Rejection without clear reason:**
> "Respected Sir/Madam, My UAN is [Your UAN]. I applied to transfer my PF from my old company [Old Company PF No] to my new company [New Company PF No]. The claim was rejected by the field office on [Date] with a vague remark. Kindly provide the exact discrepancy details so I can submit the required Joint Declaration or documents. Please assist."

### Step-by-Step Guide: How to File a Grievance on EPFiGMS

[Visit epfigms.gov.in] ➔ [Register Grievance > PF Member] ➔ [Enter UAN & OTP] ➔ [Select PF Office & Category] ➔ [Describe Issue & Attach PDF] ➔ [Submit & Get Registration Number]


1. Go to the official grievance portal: **`epfigms.gov.in`**.
2. Click on the **"Register Grievance"** tab on the top left.
3. Select the status as **"PF Member"**.
4. Enter your 12-digit UAN and security code. Click on **"Get Details"**.
5. Click on **"Get OTP"**. An OTP will be sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
6. Once authenticated, select the specific **PF Account Number (Member ID)** against which you have a grievance.
7. Choose the grievance category (e.g., "Final Settlement/Withdrawal" or "Transfer of PF Accumulations").
8. Paste the text from **Template 3** into the description box.
9. **Crucial Step:** Upload a PDF document (under 1MB) containing a screenshot of the pending claim and a copy of your Aadhaar card to support your case.
10. Submit the form. You will receive a **Grievance Registration Number** via SMS. The EPFO generally resolves or replies to these digital grievances within 7 to 15 working days.

### 3 Tips for Faster Grievance Resolution

1. **Be Precise, Not Emotional:** The EPFO dealing assistants process hundreds of grievances daily. Do not write long emotional stories. Stick to dates, UAN, Claim ID, and the exact problem.
2. **Target the Right Office:** If you worked in multiple companies in different states, your PF accounts belong to different regional offices. Ensure you select the correct Member ID of the regional office where your funds are actually stuck.
3. **Escalate if Ignored:** If your EPFiGMS grievance is closed with an automated reply (like "Claim is under process, please wait"), do not hesitate to file an appeal or a fresh grievance tagging the **Central Provident Fund Commissioner (CPFC)** on social media (X/Twitter) using your grievance number.

A delay in PF settlement can be highly stressful, but understanding how to navigate the EPFO grievance framework puts the power back in your hands. By utilizing the EPFiGMS portal efficiently, sending polite reminders to your HR, or dispatching a well-drafted physical letter to the Regional PF Commissioner, you can cut through the red tape and expedite the transfer of your hard-earned money to your bank account.
